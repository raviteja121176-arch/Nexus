# 🌿 Nexus — Smart City Intelligence

**One city. One connected mind.**

Nexus is a futuristic smart city dashboard prototype designed to demonstrate how city information, infrastructure monitoring, public safety alerts, and civic services could be brought together in one interface.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://raviteja121176-arch.github.io/Nexus/

## ✨ Features

- **City Overview:** View sample city metrics for air quality, traffic flow, water pressure, and electricity demand.
- **Interactive City Map:** Explore a schematic city map and its service locations.
- **Public Safety Demo:** Explore simulated medical, fire, and police alert workflows.
- **Civic Services:** Demonstrate how residents might submit civic issue reports.
- **Sustainability Snapshot:** Present example indicators related to urban sustainability.
- **Responsive Interface:** A clean Mint Breeze design for a modern dashboard experience.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of Nexus is to demonstrate the concept of a connected city platform that makes urban information easier to view and understand.

## ⚠️ Prototype Limitations

Nexus is a demonstration prototype. City metrics and alert workflows are simulated and should not be treated as live operational data. Civic reports are not automatically submitted to a real municipal authority, and the emergency demo does not contact emergency services or dispatch assistance.

For a real emergency in India, call **112**.
